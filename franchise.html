Ondu Motteya Kathe – Franchise Opportunity

Franchise Owned, Franchise Operated.

OMK is inviting applications from serious business owners and investors who are interested in operating an OMK outlet under our FOFO (Franchise Owned, Franchise Operated) model.

We are looking for partners who can maintain OMK's brand identity, food quality, customer experience and operational standards.

Start your application → 7 sections · about 20–30 minutes · your answers are saved on this device as you go

Basic requirements

  • Minimum 2,000 sq. ft. outlet space.
  • Investment capacity of ₹30–50 lakh.
  • Active involvement in daily business operations.
  • Appointment of one dedicated outlet manager accountable to OMK.
  • Willingness to follow OMK's operating systems, recipes and brand guidelines.
OMK – FOFO operating structure

What OMK provides

  • OMK brand name, identity and brand guidelines.
  • Standardised menu, recipes and operating procedures.
  • Supply of OMK pre-mixes, pastes and designated centralised products.
  • Billing / POS system and inventory management support.
  • Marketing guidance and brand-level promotional support.
  • Staff sourcing / supply coordination and initial training.
  • Operational monitoring and guidance through the dedicated outlet manager.
  • Menu and food quality standardisation.

How selection works

Franchise approval is subject to verification, location assessment, financial evaluation and execution of a formal franchise agreement.

  1. ApplicationTell us about you, your finances, your property and how you'll run the outlet.
  2. VerificationWe review your application and verify the details shared.
  3. Location assessmentWe assess the proposed property and locality.
  4. Financial evaluationWe confirm investment capacity and working capital.
  5. Franchise agreementA formal FOFO franchise agreement is signed.
