Before you apply

  • Total investment budget (franchise fee + setup + initial operating funds) is typically in the ₹20–50 lakh range.
  • Sales, profits and investment recovery timelines are not guaranteed — plan reserves for a slow start.
  • Someone accountable must run daily operations and complete practical training before opening.
  • Please disclose all proposed partners and investors in your application.

How selection works

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval. Each stage is separate.

  1. ApplicationTell us about you, your plans and your outlet.
  2. InterviewA conversation with the OMK team.
  3. AssessmentPractical assessment, references and funding verification — with your consent.
  4. Site approvalWe review and approve the proposed location.
  5. Opening approvalTraining complete, standards met — let's open.
